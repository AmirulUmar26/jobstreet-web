# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
[University students using JobStreet to find internships that meet their university’s required placement start date and duration.]

## What exactly is going wrong?
[In my initial review, dedicated placement-date and duration filters were not visible, and only some listings clearly stated these details. Students need to check individual descriptions or contact employers to confirm compatibility.]

## Why does it matter?
[This additional checking makes it harder for students to compare opportunities and shortlist internships that meet their university requirements.]

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
[University students seeking internships need placements that match their university’s required start date and duration. During my initial review of JobStreet, dedicated filters for these placement conditions were not visible, and only some listings clearly stated the relevant information. Students therefore need to inspect individual job descriptions and may need to contact employers to confirm whether a placement is suitable. This additional checking makes it harder to shortlist internships that meet their university requirements.]
