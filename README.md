# Improving Internship Search on JobStreet Through Placement Requirements and Rule Based Matching

A proposed JobStreet improvement that helps students find compatible internships using structured employer placement information and rule-based matching.

## Problem statement

University students seeking internships need placements that match their university’s required start date and duration. During my initial review of JobStreet, dedicated filters for these placement conditions were not visible, and only some listings clearly stated the relevant information. Students therefore need to inspect individual job descriptions and may need to contact employers to confirm whether a placement is suitable. This additional checking makes it harder to shortlist internships that meet their university requirements.
